# Legacy Plan Migration Notice * Publication date: April 21, 2026 Thank you for your continued support of GLM Coding Plan.
To provide a more consistent subscription structure and better support future product upgrades, GLM Coding Plan will gradually migrate remaining legacy subscribers to the current plan system.
The details of this update are as follows.
## 1.
Changes and Applicability Legacy plans (without weekly usage limits) will be phased out and will no longer remain as a supported subscription option going forward.
**This update applies to users who are on a legacy plan with auto-renew enabled as of April 30, 2026 (Singapore Time).
It does not affect users who are already on the current plans.** ## 2.
Details of the Changes ### 2.1 Auto-renew for legacy plans will be turned off Starting on **April 30, 2026 (Singapore Time)**, auto-renew for eligible legacy plans will be automatically canceled.
This change **will not affect your current paid subscription period**.
Your existing plan will remain active until the end of its current billing cycle.
### 2.2 Migration support To make the migration as smooth as possible, we will provide the following migration support to eligible users.
#### 2.2.1 2 complimentary months of the equivalent online version plan We will automatically provide 2 complimentary months of the equivalent online version plan tier.
This will be issued on April 30, 2026 (Singapore Time) and will be added after your current subscription ends, with no manual action required.
| Current Legacy Plan | Transition Support | | :------------------ | :--------------------------------------- | | Legacy Lite | 2 months of the online version Lite plan | | Legacy Pro | 2 months of the online version Pro plan | | Legacy Max | 2 months of the online version Max plan | #### 2.2.2 Extra benefits when migrating to the online version plan We understand that the current plan pricing has already gone through a recent price increase.
As part of this policy, eligible users will be able to purchase the latest plan at 50% off based on the latest discounted price.
This benefit will remain available from April 30, 2026 until 3 months after the end of the user’s current legacy plan period.
During this period: * The discounted price will dynamically reflect **50%** of the latest discounted price available at the time of purchase.

What this means for you * Your current legacy subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle.
* Auto-renew for the legacy plan will be canceled on **April 30, 2026 (Singapore Time)**.
* You will automatically receive 2 complimentary months of the equivalent online version plan tier, which will begin after your current subscription ends.

### 4.
FAQ **Q: Will my current legacy plan stop immediately once auto-renew is canceled?** No.
Your current subscription will remain available through the end of the existing billing period.
**Q: Do I need to manually claim the complimentary 2 months?** No.
The migration support will be granted automatically on April 30, 2026 (Singapore Time) and added after your current plan expires.
**Q: Will the complimentary online version plan be the same tier as my current legacy plan?** Yes.
We will grant 2 months of the corresponding online version plan based on the tier of your current legacy plan.
**Q: Can the value of the 2 complimentary months be used as credit when subscribing to a new plan?** Under the current subscription mechanism, plan upgrades are only available when there is a single active personal plan on the account.
While your account holds both your current legacy plan and the complimentary plan, you will not be able to perform an upgrade.
Once the complimentary plan has taken effect and becomes the only active plan on your account, upgrades will become available again, and the offset will be handled according to the applicable rules.
